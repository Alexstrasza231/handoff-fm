# Simple, funny, readable HandoffFM

Approved by the user: keep it simple and fun, make each participant's work clear, and begin editing.

The landing view is a compact two-character stage, a one-line task title, three common actions (latest change, replay latest handoff, more), and six recent meaningful activities. Each activity names its recorded actor, operation and target; ambiguous edits retain their unknown attribution label. The stage captions are labelled fictional subtitles. Model and abbreviated instance identity remain visible; full identities are expandable.

Selecting a packet or activity opens evidence. Full history, raw JSON, identity explanations, share and preferences are progressively disclosed. Replay uses the original event prefix and recorded timing; starting the latest handoff skips unrelated earlier history without fabricating events. All existing sharing opt-ins and local service boundaries remain unchanged.

Validation covers meaningful activity filtering, wrapped prompt titles, actors and unknown edits, compact default controls, one-click evidence, keyboard/replay, reduced motion, SSE and offline export. Check the real local page at a narrow desktop-panel width. Preserve the prior example component changes.
